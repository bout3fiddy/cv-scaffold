# Fictional example provenance

Avery Quill is an invented demonstration character, not an anonymized account
of a real person's career. Lantern Studio, Mosslight Tools, Kitebox Workshop,
Westmere College and Example Learning Institute are fictional example entities;
any resemblance to an existing person or organisation is coincidental.
All dates, job titles, skills, project descriptions, degrees, credentials and
metrics in the sample were invented for this template. They are not verified
professional claims and must not be carried into a real CV without evidence.
All contact and evidence links use example.com and are illustrative only.

The example demonstrates three jobs, one degree, one credential, concise
outcome bullets, underlined evidence, blue links and outlined skill tags.
