# Profile facts

Read `ground-truth.md` and `preferences.md` when present. During first use,
create them from the templates with `python init_workspace.py`.
Ask for the minimum needed to draft: target role, identity/contact preferences,
roles and dates, contributions, education and evidence. Do not request identity
documents or confidential employer material merely to complete a CV.

Record the source of each claim: user confirmation, supplied document or public
reference. An old CV is input, not automatic confirmation of every claim.
Separate personal contribution from team results and estimates from measurements.
Keep contradictions visible until resolved; never silently choose a convenient date.
When the user corrects a fact, update the ledger and locate affected master and
role sources. Rebuild only the affected documents; do not change past submissions.

Do not put actual facts into the tracked worksheet or fictional provenance file.
Private files and evidence stay ignored unless the user chooses another policy.
